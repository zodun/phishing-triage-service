# Invoice Email

## Register

product

## Users

A person managing customer invoices from their Gmail account on their own computer.
They need to identify the billed customer and prepare a payment follow-up accurately.

## Product Purpose

Read real email attachments and turn overdue invoice details into a reviewable
customer reminder. Successful setup and trustworthy source review are essential.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, approachable. The user has asked for a more engaging interface
than the original plain form. Visual direction is a working design choice, not
an established brand identity.

## Anti-references

An undifferentiated stack of form fields, unexplained configuration errors, blank
draft controls before an invoice is selected, or technical JSON as the main view.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious, especially before Gmail is configured.
- Keep the source invoice and customer draft close together.
- Show actual setup status; never invent connected accounts or financial totals.
- Make review and editing easier than automatic sending.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, keyboard focus, labeled controls, reduced motion, and a stacked
layout on smaller screens are baseline implementation requirements.
